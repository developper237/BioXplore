1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to website owners.

Cookies cannot harm your device or access other files stored on it. They are simply data files that help websites deliver a better, more personalised experience.

BioXplore uses cookies strictly to make our platform function correctly and to understand how visitors use the site — never to build advertising profiles or sell your data to third parties.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services that appear on our pages. We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics — measures site usage and visitor behaviour (anonymised). Google Privacy Policy →
  • Stripe / Payment Processors — fraud prevention cookies set during checkout. These are essential for secure transactions.
  • Font providers — Google Fonts may set cookies for performance optimisation.

We do not use social media tracking pixels or advertising networks. We do not sell data to advertisers.

6. Browser Controls

In addition to the preference panel above, you can manage or delete cookies directly through your browser. Here's how:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

Note that disabling essential cookies will prevent login, cart functionality, and checkout from working correctly.

Your continued use of BioXplore after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
