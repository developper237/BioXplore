Returns Policy

We stand behind every product we sell. If something isn't right, we'll make it right — quickly and fairly.

Last updated: February 2025

Because we ship living organisms, our returns policy is necessarily different from a standard e-commerce store. Please read the guidelines below carefully. When in doubt, contact us — we're always happy to find a fair solution.

1. Return Eligibility

To be eligible for a return or refund, please review the conditions below:

Eligible for Return

  • Wrong item sent by BioXplore
  • Item arrives dead or critically injured (within 2 hours of delivery)
  • Non-living items in original, unopened condition within 14 days
  • Significantly mis-described products
  • Duplicate or accidental orders (before dispatch)
  • Packaging kits or accessories unused and sealed

Not Eligible for Return

  • Live animals that died after confirmed delivery (unless DOA)
  • Change of mind on live organism purchases
  • Items returned without prior authorisation
  • Products damaged due to improper care after delivery
  • Digital products or downloaded educational content
  • Items returned more than 14 days after delivery

2. How to Start a Return

Follow these steps to initiate a return or claim:

1

Contact us within the claim window

Email returns@bioxplore.com or WhatsApp us. For live animals, you must contact us within 2 hours of delivery. For non-living items, within 14 days.

2

Provide your order details and evidence

Share your order number, a description of the issue, and clear photos or video of the item and packaging. For DOA claims, video evidence is strongly recommended.

3

Receive a Return Merchandise Authorisation (RMA)

We will review your claim within 1 business day and provide an RMA number along with return instructions if applicable. Do not send items back without an RMA.

4

Return the item (if required)

Ship the item using a tracked method. Return shipping costs are covered by BioXplore for errors on our part; otherwise, the customer bears the cost.

5

Receive your refund or replacement

Once we inspect the returned item or verify your claim, we will process your refund or send a replacement within 3–5 business days.

3. Live Animal Returns

Due to animal welfare considerations, we do not accept live animals back in the mail. For live organism claims:

  • Contact us within 2 hours of the first delivery attempt with photo or video evidence
  • Do not dispose of the animal — keep it in the original packaging if safe to do so
  • Our team will assess the claim and offer either a replacement or a full refund
  • We may ask you to humanely euthanise or retain the specimen at your discretion

Live arrival is guaranteed only when someone is present to accept the first delivery attempt. We cannot process claims for packages that were left unattended for extended periods in adverse conditions.

4. Non-Living Products

For non-living products such as terrariums, substrate, feeding supplies, books, and accessories:

  • Items must be returned in original, unused, and unopened condition
  • Return window: 14 days from the date of delivery
  • Include all original packaging, accessories, and documentation
  • Items showing signs of use, damage, or missing components will not be accepted

5. Refund Process

Once your return or claim is approved:

  • Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days of approval
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method
  • You will receive an email confirmation when your refund is processed
  • Bank processing times may add 2–5 additional business days depending on your institution

Original shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to a BioXplore error. If your order qualified for free shipping but falls below the free threshold after a partial return, the applicable shipping fee will be deducted from your refund.

6. Exchanges

We are happy to exchange non-living items for a different size, variant, or product of equal or lesser value. For higher-value exchanges, you will be charged the difference.

To request an exchange, follow the same process as a return (Section 2) and specify your preferred replacement in the initial message.

7. Order Cancellations

You may cancel an order before it has been dispatched at no charge. To cancel:

  • Contact us as soon as possible by WhatsApp or email
  • Include your order number and the reason for cancellation
  • A full refund will be issued if the order has not entered the packing stage

Once an order has been dispatched, it falls under our standard return policy. Live animal orders cannot be cancelled after they have been packed for dispatch.

8. Exceptions & Special Cases

We understand that exceptional situations arise. If your case does not fit neatly into the categories above — for example, a transit delay that severely stressed a live animal — please contact us. We evaluate each case individually and always aim to find a fair resolution.

BioXplore reserves the right to refuse returns that do not meet the above criteria or that we believe to be fraudulent.

Need to make a claim?

Our customer service team responds within hours. The faster you reach out, the faster we can help.
