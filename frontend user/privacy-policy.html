This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected through our website, mobile application, and any related services offered by BioXplore. By using our platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

When you create an account, place an order, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Full name, email address, and phone number
  • Shipping and billing address
  • Payment information (processed securely via third-party providers — we never store card numbers)
  • Profile preferences and account settings
  • Messages or inquiries you send us

Information collected automatically

When you browse BioXplore, we automatically collect certain technical data:

  • IP address, browser type, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent, and referring URLs
  • Device identifiers and session data
  • Geographic location (country/city level only)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Order fulfilment — processing purchases, arranging delivery, and sending order confirmations
  • Account management — creating and maintaining your BioXplore account
  • Customer support — responding to questions, complaints, and requests
  • Personalisation — recommending species and products based on your interests
  • Communications — sending transactional emails and, with your consent, newsletters
  • Legal compliance — meeting our obligations under applicable laws (CITES, GDPR, etc.)
  • Security — detecting and preventing fraud or unauthorised access

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share it only with:

  • Shipping partners — couriers and logistics providers necessary to deliver your orders
  • Payment processors — Stripe or similar PCI-DSS-compliant services
  • Analytics providers — aggregated, anonymised data only (e.g. Google Analytics)
  • Legal authorities — when required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety

All third-party partners are contractually bound to handle your data with the same level of care and confidentiality we apply.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience:

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function (login sessions, cart)
  • Performance cookies — help us understand how visitors use the site
  • Preference cookies — remember your settings and language
  • Marketing cookies — used only with your explicit consent

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account data — retained while your account is active, plus 2 years after deletion
  • Order records — retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance
  • Support communications — retained for 3 years
  • Analytics data — retained in anonymised form indefinitely

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion — request erasure of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to certain types of processing, including direct marketing
  • Restriction — request that we limit how we use your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@bioxplore.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • TLS/SSL encryption for all data transmitted to and from our servers
  • Passwords stored using bcrypt hashing — never in plain text
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Strict access controls — staff access personal data only when necessary

While we take every reasonable precaution, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

BioXplore is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "last updated" date at the top and notify registered users by email for material changes. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
