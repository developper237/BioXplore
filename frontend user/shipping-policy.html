1. Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1 to 3 business days after payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the next business day.

For live organisms, we carefully choose dispatch days (typically Monday–Wednesday) to avoid shipments sitting in transit over weekends, minimising stress to the animals.

Each living specimen is health-checked and prepared with species-appropriate substrate, moisture levels, and insulation before dispatch. This care takes time — and it's worth it.

3. Live Animal Shipping

Shipping live organisms requires special handling. We go to great lengths to ensure every specimen arrives healthy:

  • Insulated, breathable boxes designed for live cargo
  • Heat or cold packs included seasonally, based on your location and the species
  • Species-appropriate substrate (moss, cork, paper towel) to maintain humidity
  • Vented, escape-proof inner containers
  • "Live Animals" labelling on all exterior packaging

Live arrival is guaranteed when the recipient is available to receive the package on the first delivery attempt. We cannot guarantee live arrival for packages that require multiple delivery attempts or are refused at the door.

Live Arrival Guarantee

If your organism arrives dead or in critical condition upon first delivery, contact us within 2 hours with photos/video. We will issue a replacement or full refund at our discretion. Claims received after 2 hours cannot be processed.

4. International Orders

We ship to over 40 countries. International orders may be subject to:

  • Import duties, taxes, and customs fees — the responsibility of the buyer
  • CITES export documentation for applicable species (included at no extra charge)
  • Health certificates where required by the destination country
  • Longer transit times depending on customs clearance

Please verify that importing your chosen species is legal in your country before ordering. BioXplore cannot be held responsible for packages seized by customs due to incorrect import declarations or prohibited species restrictions.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via email and WhatsApp. You can use this number on the carrier's website to monitor your shipment in real time.

If your tracking shows no movement for more than 5 business days, please contact us and we will investigate with the carrier on your behalf.

6. Undeliverable Packages

If a package is returned to us as undeliverable due to an incorrect address, failed delivery attempts, or refusal of the parcel, we will contact you to arrange reshipment. Additional shipping fees will apply. BioXplore cannot refund live organisms returned in this manner.

7. Damaged or Lost Shipments

If your package arrives visibly damaged, please:

  • Take photos of the outer packaging before opening
  • Document any damage to the contents
  • Contact us within 24 hours of delivery

For lost shipments, please wait 5 business days beyond the estimated delivery date before reporting. We will file a claim with the carrier and either resend your order or issue a refund once the claim is resolved.
