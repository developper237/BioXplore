Our Story

Born from a Love of Life in All Its Forms

BioXplore began in 2019 when a group of biologists, conservationists, and educators grew frustrated with two problems they kept running into: rare specimens were either inaccessible to researchers, or they were being traded through channels with no regard for the welfare of species or ecosystems.

We built BioXplore to be different — a platform where every specimen, culture, or educational kit is sourced with full legal and ethical compliance, where documentation is transparent, and where a portion of every sale funds active conservation work.

Today, BioXplore serves researchers, educators, and passionate collectors across more than 25 countries, with a catalogue of over 500 rare species and a growing network of partner conservation organisations.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Six principles guide every decision we make — from which specimens we list to how we package and ship them.

Ethical Sourcing

Every specimen is sourced in full compliance with CITES regulations and local wildlife laws. We trace every item back to its origin and refuse to list anything we cannot verify.

Scientific Rigour

Taxonomy, habitat data, and conservation status are verified by our in-house biologists. We do not tolerate misidentification — accuracy is a matter of scientific integrity.

Education First

We believe knowledge is the most powerful tool for conservation. Every listing includes detailed educational materials, and a portion of proceeds funds school outreach programmes.

Global Access

Rare biological knowledge should not be locked behind institutional walls. We serve independent researchers, educators, and enthusiasts with the same rigour we apply to universities.

Partnership

We do not work in isolation. Our network of 50+ conservation partners ensures that our activity supports — rather than undermines — the ecosystems we care about.

Sustainability

From biodegradable packaging to carbon-offset shipping, we work to minimise our environmental footprint at every step of the supply chain.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

1
2019

BioXplore Founded

Four biologists launch BioXplore from a shared office in Accra with 47 species listings and a determination to prove ethical trade is possible at scale.

2
2020

First Conservation Partnership

We sign our first formal conservation partnership with a rainforest reserve in Cameroon, funding habitat protection through a percentage of every sale.

3
2021

Species Library Launched

Our free, publicly accessible Species Library goes live with educational content for 200+ species — the first of its kind combining scientific rigour with public accessibility.

4
2022

500 Species Milestone

The BioXplore catalogue reaches 500 listed species. We launch our school outreach programme, donating educational kits to 50 underfunded institutions.

5
2024

$500K Conservation Funded

Cumulative conservation funding through BioXplore sales crosses the half-million dollar mark across 25 partner projects in 14 countries.

6
2025

Platform 2.0 & Global Expansion

We launch the redesigned BioXplore platform with a full marketplace, live order tracking, and expanded international shipping to 40+ countries.
